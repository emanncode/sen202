# SIM Project — Community Needs Questionnaire (Google Form Design)

This questionnaire is designed to be built in **Google Forms**. It has one shared
opening section, then three branches (using Google Forms' *Sections* +
*"Go to section based on answer"* feature) — one each for **Students/Villamates**,
**Staff**, and **Landlords** — because each group experiences campus security
differently.

Form title: **"Campus Security: Help Us Design a Better Reporting System"**
Form description: "This 3–5 minute survey helps us understand how security incidents
are currently reported (or go unreported) around campus, so we can design SIM — a
platform to make reporting faster and safer. Your responses can be anonymous."

---

## Section 0 — Screening (all respondents)

1. **What is your relationship to the campus?** *(Multiple choice, required)*
   - Student living on campus
   - Student living off campus (villa/lodge)
   - Staff (academic or non-academic)
   - Landlord/property owner near campus
   - Other

   *(Set "Go to section based on answer": Student options → Section 1; Staff → Section 2; Landlord → Section 3)*

2. **Age range** *(Multiple choice, optional)* — Under 18 / 18–24 / 25–34 / 35+

3. **Gender** *(Multiple choice, optional, include "Prefer not to say")*

---

## Section 1 — Students / Villamates

4. Have you ever witnessed or experienced a security incident on or around campus? *(Yes/No)*
5. If yes, what type? *(Checkboxes)* — Theft/stealing, Assault/stabbing, Sexual assault/rape, Harassment, Burglary, Other
6. Did you report it to anyone? *(Multiple choice)* — Yes, to security / Yes, to school authority / Yes, to police / No, I didn't report it / Not applicable
7. **If you did not report it, why not?** *(Checkboxes)* — Feared retaliation, Didn't know who to report to, Didn't think anything would be done, Wanted to avoid stigma, Didn't have time, Other
8. How would you prefer to report an incident? *(Multiple choice)* — Mobile app/website, Phone call, In person, Anonymous tip box, Other
9. Would you use a platform that lets you report anonymously and track the status of your report? *(Linear scale 1–5, Not at all → Definitely)*
10. Where do you feel least safe on/around campus? *(Short answer)*
11. What features would make you trust and use a security reporting app? *(Checkboxes)* — Anonymity, Real-time alerts, Photo evidence upload, Status tracking, Map of recent incidents, SOS/emergency button, Other

---

## Section 2 — Staff

4. Have you witnessed or been informed of a security incident involving students/staff on campus? *(Yes/No)*
5. What is the current process (that you know of) for reporting a security concern? *(Short answer)*
6. How effective do you consider the current reporting process? *(Linear scale 1–5, Very ineffective → Very effective)*
7. What are the biggest gaps in how the school currently handles security incident reports? *(Checkboxes)* — Slow response, No feedback to reporter, Poor coordination with police, Lack of awareness of channels, Understaffed security, Other
8. Would a centralized digital reporting/tracking platform improve response time and accountability? *(Yes/No/Not sure)*
9. What safeguards would you want in place before adopting a reporting app for the school? *(Short answer)*

---

## Section 3 — Landlords

4. Do you own or manage student housing/lodges near campus? *(Yes/No)*
5. Have there been security incidents at your property involving student tenants? *(Yes/No/Not sure)*
6. How do tenants currently report concerns to you? *(Multiple choice)* — Phone call, In person, Text/WhatsApp, They don't report to me, Other
7. Would you support a platform where tenants can report incidents that also notifies you and campus security? *(Yes/No/Maybe)*
8. What concerns would you have about such a platform (e.g., false reports, privacy, liability)? *(Short answer)*
9. Would you be willing to display safety information (e.g., emergency contacts, QR code to the app) at your property? *(Yes/No/Maybe)*

---

## Closing (all respondents)

10. Any additional comments or suggestions for improving campus security reporting? *(Paragraph, optional)*
11. May we contact you for a brief follow-up? *(Yes + email field / No)*

---

### Notes for building this in Google Forms
- Use **Settings → "Convert this section into a branching form"** logic by adding
  a section break after Q1 and routing based on the answer.
- Turn on **"Collect email addresses": Off** and enable **anonymous responses** for
  Sections 1–3 to protect reporters, except the optional follow-up contact field.
- Use **Required** on all core questions except demographic/optional ones as marked above.
